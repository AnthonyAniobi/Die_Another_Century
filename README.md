## About the Game

Die Another Century is an exploration and rescue game set in a medieval castle. Take on the role of a soldier, navigate the castle and its surrounding areas, and bring the imprisoned people safely out.

## Features

- Explore castle grounds, towers, walls, and nearby areas.
- Search the castle for people who need your help.
- Lead rescued people out of danger.
- Discover a medieval setting built for exploration.
